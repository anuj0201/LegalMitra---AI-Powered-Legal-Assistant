# LegalMitra---AI-Powered-Legal-Assistant
LegalMitra is an AI-powered legal accessibility platform designed to demystify complex Indian jurisprudence, constitutional law, and legal documentation for everyday citizens, students, and legal professionals. Built with a clean, modern interface and bilingual capability, it bridges the gap between intricate legal jargon and plain, actionable understanding.

Key Features & Capabilities:
1.📄 Multilingual Document Simplifier & Vision OCR: Upload multiple legal documents (PDF, DOCX, TXT) at once. LegalMitra automatically extracts text—including scanned documents, Hindi, and regional scripts using Vision AI—and produces clear, jargon-free English summaries outlining key obligations, deadlines, and legal rights.

2.🛡️ Document Authenticity & Fraud Risk Auditor: An AI-driven compliance engine evaluates uploaded documents for legal validity under the Indian Contract Act, 1872. It flags fake agreements, prank notices, or stamp paper misuse with a dynamic Confidence Meter and automatically restricts chatbot access on high-risk (≥80%) fraudulent instruments.

3.💬 Context-Aware Document Follow-up Q&A: Ask specific follow-up questions directly beneath any simplified document. Backed by Indian constitutional jurisprudence and statutory frameworks, it explains enforceability, cancellation clauses, and legal mechanisms.

4.⚖️ Legal Advisor Chatbot: A dedicated chat companion with a Claude-style multi-session sidebar. Users can create fresh conversations, review chat history, and delete past sessions stored securely in local memory.

5.📜 Constitution Explorer: A searchable, categorized database of foundational Articles of the Indian Constitution, providing plain-language breakdowns, real-world examples, and landmark Supreme Court precedents.

6.📰 Real-Time Legal & National News Feed: Live categorized feeds covering Supreme Court rulings, legislative amendments, judicial reforms, and general Indian news.

7.🌓 User Profile & Theming: Seamless authentication via Email and One-Click Google Sign-In, real-time activity analytics, API key management, and instant Light/Dark mode toggling.

Tech Stack: Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+), HTML5, CSS3 Design System, PDF.js, OpenAI API (gpt-4o-mini), and Google News RSS.
